How to Play

Goal: place block pieces on the 9×9 board and clear full horizontal or vertical lines.

Controls

Drag a piece with the left mouse button. Drop it onto the board. The ghost preview shows where it will land.

Pieces

You get 3 pieces at a time. When all 3 have been placed, a new set of 3 appears.

Scoring

  • Clearing a full row or column gives 1 point per square.
  • If the entire cleared line is one color, it gives a multiplier equal to your currently available color count.
  • Multiple lines can clear at once.
  • Consecutive scoring placements build a combo. Each combo level adds +5 points.
  • The combo breaks after placing 2 pieces without clearing a line.

Colors

The game starts with 2 colors. Every 300 points unlocks another color. Perfect-color line clears become more valuable as more colors unlock. Rare rainbow pieces can count as any color for perfect-color lines.

Endless Game Over

Endless ends when at least one remaining piece cannot fit anywhere on the board after all line clears have resolved.

Adventure

Adventure levels have pre-placed objective blocks. Clear all objective blocks to complete the level. Fewer moves earn more stars.

  • Steel frame blocks lose their metal frame the first time they are cleared. The underlying block remains.
  • Color-locked blocks only clear when included in a perfect same-color line matching their color.
